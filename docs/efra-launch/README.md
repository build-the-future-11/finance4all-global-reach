# EFRA — review-only launch preview

Finance4All's European Finance & Research Alliance: useful introductions and optional collaboration for people interested in finance and economics across Europe. Italy is the proposed starting point, not a claim of existing country chapters.

## Scope

This directory adds a standalone HTML preview and an operating handoff only. It does not change application routes, authentication, database policies, dependency versions, deployment settings or existing release gates. No applicant data, member directory or private contact information is included. A draft pull request is not a public launch or a production deployment.

Open `index.html` locally to review the copy and responsive layout. It has no JavaScript dependencies, form submission handler, analytics or personal-data storage. Both interest links use the existing shared intake at https://tally.so/r/kbayyj. The campaign query parameters identify the entry route; they do not constitute consent or acceptance.

## What is saved elsewhere

An optional EFRA section has been saved in the existing shared Tally intake. At handoff, those changes are **unpublished**. It asks whether the person wants EFRA contact, preferred participation, languages, what would make an introduction useful, and the appropriate age/participation arrangement. EFRA-specific follow-ups are hidden and optional until the EFRA opt-in is selected. Existing assignments and application paths are preserved.

The human launch announcement is saved in the connected content manager as pending approval, text only, unscheduled. Nothing has been published on LinkedIn. The preview therefore directs people to mention EFRA in the existing contribution field until the new form section is available. Existing applicants may update their coordinator rather than reapply.

## Proposed first pilot

Start with a small 12–15-person group and aim for 3–5 introductions that both people find useful. These are planning targets, not current results. One introduction at a time; no mandatory recurring meeting and no obligation to complete a research project.

1. Confirm an actual coordinator and their intake/reply capacity.
2. Preview and test both EFRA opt-in branches in Tally, including a submission; publish only the reviewed version.
3. Review and explicitly publish the announcement separately.
4. Ask the Italy contact whether they would like to co-design the pilot. Do not assign a title or share their details without agreement.
5. Review explicit interests, language, availability and collaboration preferences. Do not infer age or location from a name or score people by school prestige.
6. Ask both people separately for permission for the specific introduction and the particular contact details to be shared. Keep evidence in a private log.
7. Introduce only after both agree and the coordinator checks the arrangement. Hold under-18 and age-unspecified direct introductions for an appropriate supervised route and any required permissions.
8. Ask whether the connection was useful before expanding the intake. Pause or decline is acceptable.

## Optional work after people meet

- **One question, two countries:** a short, sourced comparison of one narrow financial question. State definitions, dates and limitations; avoid claims that a small comparison represents all Europe.
- **Read it with me:** two people read an open paper and write a two-page note separating the authors' findings from their interpretation and unanswered questions.
- **Can someone else reproduce it?:** independently recreate a small public-data table or chart with a spreadsheet or script and report both successes and discrepancies.

Agree the scope, available time, contribution credit, data permissions and reviewer before starting. No promised publication, mentor, paid placement, funding or institutional endorsement. No live trading, brokerage access, paid services or personal financial records are needed for these starter activities.
