Your first steps

Simple enough to actually happen.

  1. Tell us a little about yourself.

    Your question, what you can bring and what kind of person you'd like to meet. No private financial information.

  2. Choose whether an introduction feels useful.

    We ask before sharing contact details. You can decline, pause or keep your existing project.

  3. Decide what comes next, together.

    Another conversation, a small project, or simply a new connection. There's no mandatory weekly meeting.

A few fair questions

Before you join

Who is this for?

Students, early-career researchers and curious people based in or studying in Europe. Nationality is not a selection criterion. We are building a Europe-focused pilot, not claiming existing chapters in every country.

Do I need to know quantitative finance?

No. Writing, economics, reading carefully, coding and asking thoughtful questions can all be useful. Share the level and languages you are comfortable with.

What about time and money?

The proposed founding pilot is free and voluntary. Choose a one-off conversation or an agreed small project. This is not a paid placement, investment service or promise of publication.

What happens to my information?

Your answers are for organizer review and contact. They do not authorize public profiles or group-chat additions. Each introduction requires agreement from both people.

Can someone under 18 take part?

Interest is welcome, but direct matching waits for an appropriate supervised arrangement and any required permissions. Under-18 and age-unspecified applicants are handled separately from adult peer introductions.

I already applied to Finance4All.

You do not need to repeat your application. Ask your existing coordinator to record your EFRA interest. Nothing automatically changes your current assignment.

What would you love to explore?

Tell us the question you would bring to the first conversation. We'll use it to understand the sort of connection you're looking for.
