What would be useful to you?

You don't have to sign up for an entire programme. Start with what you're looking for, then decide how far to take it.

MEET A PEER

A person, a shared interest, a place to start.

A coordinator looks for a relevant connection. You both choose whether to go ahead, in a language and format that works for you.

“I'm new to economics and want someone to read a paper with.”

Start with one paper and a question each. A written exchange is just as valid as a call. No project required.

FIND A COLLABORATOR

Bring the question. Find the missing contribution.

Say what you want to explore, what you can offer and what help you need. Choose a small first step together.

“I want to compare a financial question across two countries. Who would enjoy helping?”

The first result could be a shared source list, a careful chart or a short research note—not a promise to write a paper overnight.

EXCHANGE FEEDBACK

A fresh pair of eyes can change the work.

Share a small, authorized piece of work and the specific feedback you need. Agree the scope and time before reviewing.

“Does this conclusion actually follow from my evidence?”

Swap comments on an argument, spreadsheet or research plan. This is peer feedback, not investment advice or formal journal review.

Why this exists

The starting point isn't another chapter. It's finding people you'd actually enjoy working with.

A conversation in Italy made us rethink how Finance4All could bring people together in Europe. We are preparing a small founding group, with Italy as the first pilot and interest welcome from across Europe.

This is an early-stage, student-led initiative. We would rather make a few thoughtful connections than fill a group chat and leave people to figure it out.

Before you join

A few things
you should know.

Keep your current work. Bring your own interests. You don't have to become a quantitative researcher to belong.

Who is the founding network for?

People based in or studying in Europe who are interested in finance or economics: students, early-career researchers and curious beginners. Nationality and university prestige are not selection criteria.

Is this a paid programme?

The proposed founding pilot is free and voluntary. It is not an employment placement, and there are no promised mentors, publications, investment returns or institutional endorsements.

What if I only have a little time?

A one-off conversation or a small feedback exchange can be enough. State your availability. No compulsory weekly meeting or automatic project assignment.

Will my contact details become public?

No public directory is being created here. The organizers ask both people before each introduction. Joining the interest list does not authorize a group-chat addition or public profile.

Can someone under 18 participate?

You can express interest. Direct introductions wait for an appropriate supervised arrangement and any required permissions. Younger and age-unspecified applicants need a separate review.

I already applied to Finance4All. What now?

Tell your existing coordinator you are interested in EFRA. You do not need to restart an application or leave an existing assignment.
