# FinanceMeta + Finance4All Launch Kit — September 2026

This document is publication-ready operating copy for the current FinanceMeta launch. It deliberately separates **active intake**, **interest routing**, and **external relationships** so the public site does not turn plans into claims.

## Launch position

### FinanceMeta

**FinanceMeta is a student-led finance, economics, quantitative research, and financial-technology network built around work that can be inspected.**

Participants can enter through research labs, applied financial-education work, chapters, publications, or scoped build projects. A program is treated as active only when it has a real intake or operating owner; a research result is treated as evidence only when methods, assumptions, data provenance, and limitations are traceable.

Short version:

> Study financial systems. Build useful things. Show the evidence.

### Finance4All India

**Finance4All India is the applied financial-education and community-programming arm of FinanceMeta for schools, student communities, educators, and partner organizations in India.**

The India program should complement — not imitate or imply affiliation with — regulator-backed financial-literacy work. NCFE's National Strategy for Financial Education emphasizes relevant content, capacity, community-led models, communication, and collaboration, while NCFE already runs school and teacher financial-education programs. Finance4All therefore focuses on a distinct application layer: practical financial-system exercises, digital-payments and consumer-protection sessions, peer-led projects, data/policy sprints, and locally adapted student outputs.

Public claim boundary:

> Finance4All India is independent educational programming. It does not provide personalized financial or investment advice and does not imply endorsement by RBI, SEBI, NCFE, IRDAI, PFRDA, NPCI, or another regulator or institution.

Reference context:
- NCFE National Strategy for Financial Education: https://ncfe.org.in/nsfe/
- NCFE Money Smart School Program: https://ncfe.org.in/mssp/
- NCFE Money Smart Teachers Programme: https://ncfe.org.in/mstp/

## Programs to launch now

### 1. Quantitative Markets & Microstructure Lab — Cohort 01

**Public description**

Study markets as systems rather than stories: market structure, microstructure, volatility, factors, forecasting, execution, risk, robust backtesting, and financial ML. Projects use fair baselines, realistic assumptions, and reproducible analysis. Simulated or backtested performance is never presented as realized returns.

**Intake:** https://tally.so/r/0Q9qZy

**Minimum operating standard**
- one bounded question per project;
- named dataset/provenance and train/test or temporal split policy;
- simple and strong matched baselines;
- explicit leakage, transaction-cost, and regime checks where relevant;
- retained scripts/results sufficient to reproduce the reported table or figure;
- limitations and null/negative findings kept in the record.

**Candidate project directions**
- event-study robustness under alternative windows and matched controls;
- volatility forecasting under rolling versus regime-aware baselines;
- market-impact or liquidity proxies with explicit data limitations;
- factor stability and turnover under realistic rebalance assumptions;
- forecasting experiments that separate predictive signal from leakage and non-stationarity.

### 2. Financial Systems, Access & Policy Lab — Cohort 01

**Public description**

Research how financial systems affect people in the real world: credit and lending, digital payments, inclusion, consumer protection, access, policy evaluation, fairness, and financial infrastructure. India-facing projects can study questions around UPI, digital finance, household financial behaviour, access, consumer safeguards, or policy design while remaining globally relevant and evidence-led.

**Intake:** https://tally.so/r/lbG8Yk

**Candidate project directions**
- digital-payment adoption and access using public aggregate data;
- consumer-protection or scam-awareness interventions with pre/post evaluation;
- credit-access or lending-policy analysis with explicit fairness definitions;
- policy-brief replication using public datasets;
- financial-inclusion measurement and sensitivity to metric definitions;
- household decision-making experiments that avoid personalized financial advice.

### 3. Finance4All India — School & Community Pilots

**Public description**

Practical financial education for schools and communities, built around decisions and systems rather than promotional finance content. Pilot formats include interactive financial-systems workshops, digital-payments/scams/consumer-protection sessions, student data or policy sprints, peer-led programs, teacher/facilitator support, and evaluation collaborations.

**Pilot interest:** https://tally.so/r/q4rlKd

**Pilot design standard**
1. define the audience, need, and existing curriculum before proposing a session;
2. identify the institutional owner and approval/safeguarding requirements;
3. choose one measurable learning or project outcome;
4. use educational examples, not personal investment recommendations;
5. collect only data necessary for program delivery/evaluation;
6. record what actually happened before making any public reach or impact claim.

## Applicant review rubric

Score each dimension 0–4. A strong applicant does not need a polished public profile; evidence of thought and execution matters more.

| Dimension | 0 | 2 | 4 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Question quality | vague topic | plausible question | bounded, testable, decision-relevant question |
| Evidence discipline | assertion only | names data/analysis | baseline, provenance, falsifier/confounder understood |
| Execution | no evidence | partial project experience | clear ownership and completed/reviewable work |
| Technical/analytical fit | no relevant foundation | learning-ready | strong fit for chosen track |
| Intellectual honesty | overclaims | reasonable caveats | actively identifies failure modes and alternative explanations |
| Commitment | unclear | plausible | specific weekly capacity and milestone reliability |

Suggested decision bands: 19–24 strong admit; 15–18 admit/waitlist based on team balance; 11–14 project trial or foundations route; <=10 decline for this cycle with permission to reapply. The rubric is a decision aid, not an automatic acceptance rule.

## External-dependency / claim gates

Do **not** publish any of the following until direct evidence exists:
- partnership, endorsement, or backing by NCFE, RBI, SEBI, IRDAI, PFRDA, NPCI, a school, university, company, or speaker;
- participant counts derived from interest forms rather than actual participation;
- publication, internship, investment-performance, placement, or competition outcomes without linked evidence;
- a FinTech Studio cohort as active while its dedicated intake remains closed;
- a chapter as active before its approval and qualifying-output gate.
